### `baseline_at_capacity_*` is the important one

It is the **only at-capacity, non-scanning link-layer data this project has**.
Every run before N8 peaked at `linked=9` (N7, v43 night) or lower, so all of them
were scanning throughout and all of their link-layer numbers describe the
degraded state. N8 held ten connections for 2 h 13 m and the RECONNECT ended it.

`reports` 20.07–20.10 on all ten nodes, `event_gaps` 0.004–0.033/s, and **no tier
structure at all** — the two tiers are a scanning artefact.

Compare future link-layer measurements against this window, not against other
runs. Two degraded runs compared to each other cannot reveal a fleet-wide
regression, because the degradation is common-mode.

The CSVs carry `dt_BSF1120` and `dt_BSFEC35` — signed seconds from each onset —
so either board's series can be aligned without recomputing epochs. `t_run_s` is
seconds since run open (`T0 = 1786108497.195507`).

Onsets: **BSFEC35 15:46:10.53**, **BSF1120 16:53:10.54**.

## Reading the counters

`reports` is connection events per ~1005 ms window; 20 is nominal at the 50 ms
interval. `event_gaps` counts skipped events and, in this run, accounts for the
entire `reports` shortfall — so a drop in `reports` here means events not
scheduled, not packets lost. `crc_error` and `nak` stayed at their baseline
throughout, including on both wedged links.
