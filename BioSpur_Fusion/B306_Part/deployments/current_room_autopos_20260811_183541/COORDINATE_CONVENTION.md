# Coordinate convention

This deployment is `RELATIVE_GEOMETRY_ONLY`.

The frozen V4-io gauge puts Anchor A at `(0,0,0)`, B on the positive X axis,
and C in the XY plane with positive Y. The remaining sign ambiguity is a
reflection through that plane. The frozen objective contains a signed
two-layer prior; of the converged production and reflected initializations,
the lower-cost basin was selected. It places D near the A/B/C layer and
E/F/G/H about 1.69 m in positive Z. This establishes a consistent solver frame,
but there was no ultrasound, survey, or room-frame measurement in this run.
Consequently positive Z must not be described as surveyed room Z-up, and this
layout must not be transformed to a building frame without new evidence.

The selected reflected initialization cost was 97.0471 versus 130.6214 for
the opposite mirror. The two mirror solutions differ by at most 6.61 mm in
pair distance; the mirror choice therefore changes handedness, not the ranging
fit. All tag results use this exact relative frame.
