# C2CC_NATURAL_MOTION_FITTING_PASS_HELDOUT_FAIL

The existing capture is sufficient to recover independent, proper natural-motion frame bindings without treating the operator's labels as directions. Mount A accepted 30 fitting segments; Mount B accepted 19. Both passed excitation rank, measurement-aware residual, bootstrap, leave-one-segment-out, no-dominant-stroke and Q1 numerical-integrity gates before validation was opened.

After the signed freeze manifest was written, each validation block was decoded once as arbitrary motion. Neither block was internally consistent under the frozen rules. Mount A's endpoint P95 was 93.500° and normalized path P95 was 1.625; Mount B's values were 140.805° and 1.681. The frozen limits were 75° and 0.90. Q1 remained numerically healthy, both fused paths remained below 1.17 m from their origins, endpoint ZUPT reduced fused stop speed below 0.004 m/s, and subsequent gravity-norm residuals passed. The failure is therefore transform generalization to unseen motion, not state explosion or covariance corruption.

The 13.125° hand-lift-derived disagreement is preserved as historical evidence but is not a physical-up gate. Mount transforms are intentionally different and independently fitted; their relative rotation was 86.542°. Identity accelerometer calibration remains primary. The failed device-specific calibration changed fitting rotation by only 0.131°/0.168° as an oracle sensitivity and never selected the transform. The fitting-only offset sweep also disagreed across actions (-60 to +80 ms), so the frozen 0 ms common-clock policy remains correct and no per-action timing tune is admitted.

This capture does **not** authorize proceeding directly to ten-node arbitrary-wear T-pose/body calibration. First repair or replace the stop-to-stop inertial displacement model and validate it prospectively on one node with independent trajectory reference; only then scale. The present evidence does not validate body-segment anatomical axes, external trajectory accuracy, antenna/IMU lever-arm centers, or production-wide calibration transfer. Because the one-time replay did not persist pre-update innovation/NIS or a held-out oracle replay, those products were not manufactured by reopening held-out data; the limitation is recorded explicitly.
