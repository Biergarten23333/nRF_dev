# Limitations

The arm is non-rigid and has no measured radius, angle, home, revolution count, motor telemetry or external trajectory truth. No absolute RMSE, radius error, angular-speed error, loop closure, lever-arm validation or sensor-to-V4 rotation claim is available. Any plane/circle/path quantity is `NO_EXTERNAL_TRUTH_SELF_CONSISTENCY_ONLY`. Cycle 3–5 were not executed under an operator shortening chosen before analysis; therefore the pre-registered five-cycle Full PASS is unavailable.
