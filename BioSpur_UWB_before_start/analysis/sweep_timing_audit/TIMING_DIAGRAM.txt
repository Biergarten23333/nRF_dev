Broadcast Alt-SS-TWR — ONE sweep cycle, µs resolution
=====================================================
Model: 1 delayed poll -> 1 collector window -> N responders staggered by rank.
Responder delay(rank) = GUARD(1200) + rank*SPACING(1000)  [us]
Collector window(N)   = GUARD + (N-1)*SPACING + TAIL(800) - poll_airtime(335)  [us]
t = 0 at poll TX-done (TXFRS).
